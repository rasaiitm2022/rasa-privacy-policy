PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Regenerative Agriculture Stack Architecture (RASA) (“the Application”). This document outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, storage, and safeguarding of your personal data when you access or use our mobile application.

RASA is developed to support agricultural workflows, connect users across India with agricultural tools and knowledge, and streamline field data reporting. We value user trust and treat your personal data with high standards of security and transparency.

By downloading, registering, or interacting with the Application, you agree to the practices outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope and Geographic Applicability

This Application is intended primarily for users located in India. All personal data collected through the Application is processed in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, and relevant Information Technology regulations.

3. Information We Collect

We limit data collection strictly to what is essential for application stability, authentication, and core functionality.

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Account & Profile Details: Full Name and Mobile Number (used for account creation).
  • Field / Agriculture Data: Crop details, farm location parameters, or diagnostic images uploaded at your discretion.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device details (hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers).
  • Network information (IP address, connection type).
  • Application performance telemetry, crash reports, and system interaction logs.

4. App Permissions & Clear Purpose

To deliver specific agricultural tools, RASA may request access to device capabilities. Each permission is requested dynamically at runtime with a clear in-app prompt:

  • Camera: Used exclusively to photograph crops, plant leaves, and farm conditions for diagnostic inspection and community sharing.
  • Photo Library & Storage: Used strictly to let you select and attach existing crop images or relevant documents.
  • Internet & Network Access: Required to communicate securely with our cloud servers and synchronize data.
  • Push Notifications (Optional): Used to deliver operational alerts, task reminders, and community updates (can be turned off via device settings).

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers for cloud hosting, storage, and platform delivery. These providers process data strictly to fulfill operational functions under confidential, encrypted connections:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Cloud hosting, database management, and media storage.
  • Google Play Services: Fundamental Android system capabilities, in-app updates, and platform security.

6. Data Security

We implement administrative and technical security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Restricted backend database access controls.
  • Regular log audits and vulnerability monitoring.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

We retain personal information only for as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfill agricultural platform services and legal obligations.

Your Right to Delete Account and Data:
You have full control over your data. You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated records at any time:
  • In-App: Navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  • By Email: Send a request to our privacy desk at rasaiitm2022@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered contact number/email.
Upon verification, your profile details and uploaded records will be permanently purged within 15 days, except where retention is mandated by law.

8. Children's Privacy

RASA is intended for adult users, farmers, and agricultural professionals. It is not directed at or designed for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

9. Policy Revisions

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect application enhancements or statutory requirements. When updates occur, the "Effective Date" at the top of this document will be updated accordingly.
